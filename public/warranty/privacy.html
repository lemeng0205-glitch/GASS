Privacy Policy

Warranty registration · Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Lemeng Limited Company ("we", "us") handles the personal information you give us when you register a product for warranty on this page.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Lemeng Limited Company, 11F., No. 216, Fuhui Rd., Xinzhuang Dist., New Taipei City 242, Taiwan.
Contact for privacy questions: service@lemeng.com.tw.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy we need it
Name, email addressTo identify your registration and contact you about warranty or after-sales support.
Country / regionTo apply the correct warranty terms and arrange service.
Product, serial number (optional)To know which product is covered.
Where you bought it, order number, purchase dateTo confirm the purchase and work out the warranty period.

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not collect information from this page through advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To record your warranty registration and give you a registration number.
  • To verify your purchase and provide warranty and after-sales service.
  • To contact you about your registration or a service request.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you separately agree to that.

4. Legal basis

Where the law requires a legal basis (for example in the EU and the UK), we rely on your consent, given when you tick the box on the registration form, and on our need to carry out the warranty service you asked for. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY LEGAL]

5. Who we share it with

  • Service providers who store or process the data for us: Google (data storage) and our website hosting provider.
  • Authorised repair or logistics partners, only when needed to handle your service request.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. Where your information is stored

Your information may be stored and processed outside your country, including in Taiwan and the United States. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY LEGAL]

7. How long we keep it

We keep your registration for the warranty period of your product plus 1 year, then delete or anonymise it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us to show you, correct, or delete your information, to withdraw your consent, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to receive a copy of it. To make a request, email service@lemeng.com.tw. We will reply within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Withdrawing consent or asking for deletion may mean we can no longer find your registration when you ask for warranty service.

You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority in your country.

9. Security

We limit access to your information to staff who need it for warranty and after-sales work, and we send it over an encrypted connection. No system is completely secure, but we will tell you and the relevant authority if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

10. Children

This page is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
